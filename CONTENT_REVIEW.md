# Client Content Approval Required

The supplied visual design and industry positioning are preserved. Source copy is not treated as independent proof. An illustrative label has been added to each sample-report panel.

Before paid traffic, the client should approve or correct:

| Topic | Required evidence / action |
| --- | --- |
| Firefighter headline and operational-readiness implications | Confirm evidence supports the exact claims. Sweat-loss measurement must not be presented as certification that someone is safe to deploy. |
| Heat strain, hydration and electrolyte measurements | Identify what THIS patch variant directly measures, what the app estimates, calibration requirements and limitations. Do not conflate sweat loss with core temperature or clinical diagnosis. |
| ISO, skin safety, 3M adhesive and biocompatibility statements | Supply the exact standard, test report, product scope and applicable conditions. A general ISO reference is not a product certification. |
| Death counts, percentages, construction/mining statistics and heat-risk map | Verify each number, source, population, year and geographical scope. The supplied heatmap is not a verified forecasting service. |
| Sony, EU, EIT and other award/funding logos | Confirm the accurate relationship and permission to use the logos. Participation/funding must not be presented as clinical endorsement. |
| Seattle pilot and its timeline | Confirm the pilot happened as stated and that outcomes/partner names can be published; otherwise label it as a proposed/example pilot or remove the claims. |
| Mining work context | Underground messaging may not fit quarry/aggregates prospects. Check the product's actual suitability; do not imply intrinsically safe certification without evidence. |
| Sample report and demonstration values | Clearly illustrative; do not represent invented crew data as measured customer outcomes. |
| 10-minute call | Sirisha owns correction of the actual Calendly duration; the website does not change her event. |
| Price / offer | Introduction is free. Organizational pilots are paid; confirm their scope, commercial terms and procurement route. |

These are launch approval items, not findings that every statement is false. No performance, clinical-safety, liability-reduction or certification guarantee is added by this implementation.
